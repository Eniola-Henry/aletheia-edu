# Aletheia.edu — 2-minute demo script

**Total: ~120 seconds**

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem
On screen: textbook / dense notes (or just say it).
> "Students who learn through stories shut down on long pages of facts. Chatbots explain — but they don’t check understanding, and they don’t feel like something you’d *finish*."

## 0:15–0:40 — Solution in product
Screen: Landing → Begin → Dashboard.
> "Aletheia turns a curriculum into a black-and-white comic. Pick a subject — or upload your own PDF. Choose a genre and a character."

Show: subject card, genre (Fantasy Quest), archetype Alex → **Forge course**.

## 0:40–1:15 — The loop
Screen: Story map → Read chapter → Reader Mode (click panels).
> "You read a short comic, fact-anchored to real topics. Then one Socratic question."

Submit a **weak** answer → show rubric + amber correction.
> "If you miss the point, you get a real correction — not empty praise. Explain more if you need it. Try again."

## 1:15–1:45 — Why AI is core
> "A multi-agent pipeline mines topics, builds a narrative arc, writes the comic, and grades against the same facts. Genre changes tone *and* location. PDF upload builds a course from *your* notes."

Optional: flash PDF topic preview.

## 1:45–2:00 — Close
Story map with progress + best scores.
> "Aletheia isn’t another story generator. It’s a comic you finish — and a check that you understood it."
